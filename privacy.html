TECHWORLD24 - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Introduction

TECHWORLD24 respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you visit our website or interact with our content and social media accounts.

2. Information We Collect

Our website does not currently offer user registration, account creation or direct payment services.

Depending on how you interact with our website and social media content, information may be processed by hosting providers and third-party platforms. This may include technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and access logs.

If you contact us, we may process the information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address and message.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain our website.
  • Respond to messages and inquiries.
  • Manage and publish digital content.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of our services.
  • Understand how our content is accessed and used.

4. Social Media Platforms

TECHWORLD24 may publish and manage content through third-party platforms, including TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.

These platforms process personal information according to their own privacy policies. When users interact with our profiles or content, the relevant platform may collect and process information independently.

Where authorized integrations are used to manage publishing, information necessary to operate those integrations may be processed in accordance with the permissions granted.

5. Cookies and Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and other service providers may process technical information necessary to deliver and secure the website.

Third-party websites may use their own cookies and tracking technologies. Please consult their privacy policies for further details.

6. Affiliate Links

Our content may contain affiliate links. If you follow an affiliate link, the third-party provider may use cookies or similar technologies to attribute a purchase or referral.

TECHWORLD24 does not control the data collection practices of third-party providers.

7. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable under data protection laws, personal information is processed on the basis of legitimate interests, consent, contractual necessity or legal obligations, depending on the circumstances.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by hosting providers, social media platforms and service providers when necessary to operate our services or comply with legal obligations.

9. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, restrict or object to processing, and request data portability.

Individuals in the European Economic Area may also have the right to lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and content are not intentionally directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

12. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information under our control. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

14. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the TECHWORLD24 team through its official contact channel.
